---
description: Draft a customer message to drafts/: an overdue reminder, a permit-ending note, or a price review letter after a tip rate rise. Never sends.
---

1. Run one of: `node scripts/waste.mjs draft overdue INV-...`, `draft permit H-...`, `draft price "CUSTOMER"`.
2. Open the file in `drafts/` and read it to the operator. Edit in their voice if they ask.
3. A person sends it from their own email. Nothing here sends.
